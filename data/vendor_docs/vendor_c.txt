Vendor C Information Security Policy Document

Encryption:
Vendor C enforces strict AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Key rotation is automated every 30 days.

Access Control:
Strict zero-trust architecture is in place. MFA is required for all access, and physical security at data centers is guarded 24/7.

Incident Response:
We lack a dedicated incident response team. In case of an incident, the IT support desk will notify affected customers within 7 days.

Compliance & Certifications:
Fully compliant with ISO 27001 and GDPR. PCI-DSS compliance is maintained and audited annually.

Vulnerability Management:
Continuous automated scanning is deployed. Patches for critical zero-days are applied within 24 hours.
