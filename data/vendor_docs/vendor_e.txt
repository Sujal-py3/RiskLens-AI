Vendor E Security Whitepaper

Encryption:
AES-256 for all data at rest. All communications over public networks use TLS 1.2 or higher.

Access Control:
MFA is mandatory. We employ principle of least privilege (PoLP) and conduct quarterly access reviews.

Incident Response:
Highly mature SOC operates 24/7. Incident response plan is tested quarterly. Breach notification is guaranteed within 24 hours.

Compliance & Certifications:
SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and fully PCI-DSS certified (Level 1 Service Provider).

Vulnerability Management:
Weekly vulnerability scans and annual manual penetration testing. Critical patches applied within 48 hours.
