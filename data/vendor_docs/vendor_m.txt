Vendor M Payment Processing Addendum

Encryption:
Tokenization is used for all credit card data. Original PANs are never stored.

Access Control:
Strict network segmentation isolates the cardholder data environment (CDE). Jump hosts with MFA are required to access the CDE.

Incident Response:
Dedicated incident response team. PagerDuty alerts for any suspicious CDE activity.

Compliance & Certifications:
PCI-DSS Level 1 Service Provider. Attestation of Compliance (AoC) is available upon request.

Vulnerability Management:
Weekly internal and external ASV scans. All vulnerabilities in the CDE are patched immediately.
