Vendor D Third-Party Risk Assessment Summary

Encryption:
Data at rest is not encrypted due to legacy system constraints. We plan to migrate to a new encrypted system by Q4 next year. Transit encryption uses TLS 1.1.

Access Control:
Basic password policies are enforced (minimum 8 characters). MFA is not currently supported by our core platform.

Incident Response:
Documented incident response plan exists and is tested bi-annually. Notification to clients occurs within 48 hours of confirmed breach.

Compliance & Certifications:
Vendor D does not hold PCI-DSS or SOC2 certifications. 

Vulnerability Management:
No formal vulnerability scanning is performed. We rely on external bug bounties to identify security flaws.
