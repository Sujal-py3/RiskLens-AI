Vendor G Cloud Security Addendum

Encryption:
Cloud provider default encryption is used for data at rest. We do not manage our own encryption keys.

Access Control:
Federated SSO via Okta is used for all systems, enforcing MFA and conditional access based on geographic location.

Incident Response:
Standardized playbook exists for ransomware, data exfiltration, and DDoS. Tested annually. 

Compliance & Certifications:
SOC2 Type II compliant. PCI-DSS is maintained by our payment gateway sub-processor, not us directly.

Vulnerability Management:
Monthly scans. Medium/Low vulnerabilities are often accepted as risk and not patched.
